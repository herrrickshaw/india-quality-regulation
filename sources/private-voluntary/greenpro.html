9th Edition GreenPro Summit 2026,

The 9th Edition CII GreenPro Summit 2026, held on 30–31 July 2026 in Pune, brought together industry leaders, policymakers, sustainability experts and manufacturers to accelerate green products and low-carbon value chains.
With the theme “Green Products Driving Decarbonization in Value Chains,” the summit focused on sustainable manufacturing, green procurement, circularity, resource efficiency and decarbonisation.
Key discussions highlighted the role of GreenPro-certified products, responsible procurement and innovation in reducing carbon emissions across industries.
The summit also recognised 143 organisations through the GreenPro Awards for their contributions to environmentally preferable products and sustainable manufacturing.

Launch of GreenPro Ecolabel for Facility Management Services at GreenPro Summit 2024

The launch of the GreenPro Ecolabel for Facility Management Services, under leadership of Dr. C. Velan, represents a groundbreaking initiative to enhance sustainability in the industry. Developed with the expertise of professionals from major organizations such as CBRE, JLL, Colliers, and Siemens, this ecolabel establishes rigorous criteria for energy efficiency, waste management, and resource conservation. By promoting collaboration among leading companies, the standard not only builds consumer confidence but also encourages continuous improvement in eco-friendly practices. As organizations increasingly prioritize sustainability, the GreenPro Ecolabel positions facility management services to meet this demand while reducing their environmental footprint and differentiating themselves in a competitive market.

Accreditation By Global Ecolabelling Network

CII has received accreditation for its GreenPro Ecolabel certification from Global Ecolabelling Network (GEN) through GENICES – GEN’s Internationally Coordinated Ecolabelling System. Global Ecolabelling Network is a network of Ecolabelling Organisations that advocates for Type 1 Ecolabel based on ISO 14024 standards and articulates their credibility.

The accreditation by GEN:

  • Assures that GreenPro Ecolabel certification is at par with international standards and protocols for green product labeling.
  • Creates an excellent opportunity for GreenPro Ecolabelled products to gain international recognition.
  • Facilitates enhanced global market outreach and create export opportunities

Launch Of GreenPro Ecolabelling Standard For Solar PV Module At Green Power Conference

CII GreenPro with support of all stakeholders including product manufacturers, testing laboratories, consultants and end users has developed GreenPro Ecolabelling standard for solar PV module to enable the end users to select credible high performing rooftop solar photovoltaic modules. The ecolabel will be based on performance testing of solar photovoltaic modules by a 3rd party laboratories and verifiers. The GreenPro Ecolabelling standard was released by Mr Ramesh Kymal Chairman, RE Council – CII Godrej GBC and CMD, Siemens Gamesa Renewable Power Pvt. Ltd at 18 th edition of Green Power Conference, Chennai.

CII GreenPro, IFC, IPA And IAPMO Join Hands To Promote Water Efficient Plumbing Fixtures In India

CII GreenPro in association with the Indian Plumbing Association and International Association of Plumbing and Mechanical Officials (IAPMO) has developed the GreenPro Ecolabel Certification standard for Water-Efficient Plumbing Fixtures, Sanitaryware, Clothes Washer and Dish Washers. This has been done with the support of all stakeholders including product manufacturers, testing laboratories, consultants and end-users.
The GreenPro certification standard was released by Mr. Tuyen D. Nguyen, Principal Operations Officer, IFC at 3rd edition of GreenPro Summit, Pune.

About GreenPro

GreenPro is a Type – 1 Ecolabel which enables the end users in the building sector and manufacturing industries to choose right products, materials and technologies for reducing the environment impacts during the construction, operation and maintenance of their buildings and factories.

GreenPro Ecolabel is accredited by Global Ecolabelling Network (GEN) through GENICES – GEN’s Internationally Coordinated Ecolabelling System.
