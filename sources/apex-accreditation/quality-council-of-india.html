About QCI

Quality Council of India (QCI) is a premier autonomous body set up by the Government of India. QCI is responsible for creating a Quality Mindset and envisions to ensure quality across products and services that touch every citizen. As an independent and autonomous body, QCI creates a mechanism for independent third-party assessments of products, services, and processes, coordinating its activities through its constituent boards and divisions. The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry, serves as the nodal point for QCI.

Nation Building Initiatives

Our initiatives bring quality into action across sectors.

Quality Council of India leads national initiatives to strengthen quality, build institutional capacity, and support India’s development priorities. Through programmes such as Gunvatta Gurukul, Sarpanch Samvaad, and sector-focused quality interventions, QCI promotes adoption of standards, improves service delivery, and enhances stakeholder competence.
