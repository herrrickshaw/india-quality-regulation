About NABET

About NABET The National Accreditation Board for Education and Training (NABET), a constituent board of Quality Council of India (QCI), is dedicated to promoting excellence in education, training, and skill development across India. As a trusted accreditation body in India, NABET QCI delivers strong accreditation and assessment systems aligned with both national and international standards, playing a crucial role in improving institutional quality and supporting competency-based learning.

With a strong emphasis on quality assurance and ongoing improvement, NABET enables institutions to benchmark their performance, strengthen their systems, and adapt to changing industry and academic standards. Its initiatives play a vital role in developing a skilled, competent, and future-ready workforce.

NABET is chaired by distinguished industrialist and educationist Mr. Jalaj Dani, whose leadership continues to guide the Board in advancing quality standards and fostering excellence across the education and training ecosystem.

Operating through its three core divisions, Education (ED), Environment & Exploration (EED), and Training & Capacity Building (TCB), NABET aligns quality assurance, capacity development, and regulatory efforts to promote systemic improvements and a future-ready ecosystem.

The Education Division (ED) bolsters the education system through large-scale initiatives focused on improving learning outcomes, increasing institutional effectiveness, and ensuring Quality Council of India accreditation standards across school, tribal, medical, technical, and higher education. Its activities include school accreditation, enhancements in teaching and learning, national scheme evaluations, as well as assessments, rankings, and audits across higher and professional education. This helps strengthen institutional performance and accountability.

The Environment & Exploration Division (EED) enhances environmental governance by accrediting EIA Consultant Organisations and expanding into exploration- and mining-related accreditation frameworks such as AEA, APA, and MPPA. It encourages compliance, transparency, and responsible development across industrial and infrastructure sectors.

Through the Training & Capacity Building Division (TCB), NABET enhances professional skills across key sectors with tailored and scalable training solutions. Collectively, the divisions position NABET as a driver for quality, accountability, and systemic improvement aligned with national and global priorities.

Vision

To develop an ecosystem of credible accreditation mechanisms in education & training and create an environment to achieve the defined SDGs.

Mission

To foster sustainable growth for all stakeholders in the field of education, training, skills & services by way of qualitative interventions and an effective, accountable & independent accreditation structure.
