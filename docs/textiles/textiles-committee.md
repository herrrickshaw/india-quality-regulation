# Textiles Committee

**Authority:** Ministry of Textiles (statutory body)
**Status:** Mixed
**Established:** 1963 (Act); began functioning 22 August 1964
**Legal basis:** Textiles Committee Act, 1963 (Act No. 41 of 1963)
**Marks issued:** No product mark of its own — it administers/licenses the [Handloom Mark](handloom-mark.md) and issues pre-shipment inspection certificates for textile exports

## Scope

The Textiles Committee is a statutory body corporate created by Parliament under the Textiles Committee Act, 1963, to ensure the quality of textiles and textile machinery, both domestically and for export. It operates a network of testing laboratories that check fibre content, dyeing, finishing and construction against Indian and international standards, and it runs mandatory pre-shipment quality inspection for specified textile and textile-machinery exports — the "Mixed" status in this repo reflects that some of its functions (export inspection where notified) carry compliance force while others (general testing services, market research) are commercial/advisory. Beyond testing, the Committee is the implementing agency for the [Handloom Mark](handloom-mark.md) scheme, licensing weavers and weaver societies to use the authenticity label. It functions under the administrative control of the Ministry of Textiles, distinct from but complementary to the [BIS](../bis-core/) testing infrastructure — BIS covers general product standards under the BIS Act, while the Textiles Committee's mandate is textiles-specific and includes export compliance.
